Privacy Policy

How WoS: Kelime Çarkı Bulmaca handles your data.

Who we are and how to reach us

The game “WoS: Kelime Çarkı Bulmaca” (the “game”) is published by Hüseyin Çınar, trading as CNRGAMES (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

What data we collect

  • Anonymous player ID. When the game starts it signs you in automatically with Unity Authentication and creates a random player ID. It does not contain your name or e-mail address.
  • Optional account sign-in. If you choose “Sign In” instead of playing as a guest, the sign-in is run by Unity Player Accounts (Google, Apple or e-mail and password). We never see your password. Your sign-in identity is linked to your player ID so that you can restore your progress on another device.
  • Game data (Cloud Save). Your progress in each season and level, your settings (language, region, music and sound volume, vibration), and your premium status: the dates of the welcome trial, and the product and expiry date of any pass or subscription.
  • Purchase data. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. To unlock premium access, the game sends the purchase receipt to our server-side validation (Unity Cloud Code), which checks it with Google. We keep a record of the store transaction ID together with your player ID so that one purchase cannot be used by several players.
  • App configuration requests. When the game starts it asks Unity Remote Config for its current settings and sends your player ID, the language and region chosen in the game, the app version and the platform. Season content is downloaded from Unity Cloud Content Delivery.
  • Technical connection data. Whenever your device contacts a server, that server receives your IP address. The game also checks that you are online by sending a simple request to a public web server (currently www.google.com).

Why we use your data and on what basis

  • To provide the game: sign-in, saving and restoring your progress, season content, premium access. Basis: performance of the contract with you (the Terms of Service).
  • To process and validate purchases and to prevent misuse of purchase receipts. Basis: performance of the contract, our legitimate interest in preventing fraud, and our legal obligations for accounting and tax records.
  • To keep the game working and secure (configuration, maintenance, forced updates). Basis: our legitimate interest.
  • You confirm in the game that you accept this policy and the Terms of Service. We keep that confirmation on your device (see below).

What we do not collect

The game contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, and it does not use your advertising ID. It does not use analytics or tracking services. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and it does not collect your real name, address or payment card details.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask you to accept the new version before it applies.

Data stored only on your device

The game stores on your device: your settings (language, region, volume, vibration), a cached copy of your progress, downloaded season content, cached configuration values, and your confirmation that you accepted this policy and the Terms of Service (version, date, and your age confirmation). This data does not leave your device unless it is part of the game data described above.

Processors and international transfers

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services) provides Authentication, Player Accounts, Cloud Save, Remote Config, Cloud Code and Cloud Content Delivery. Privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google provides Google Play (purchases and subscriptions) and receives the connectivity check described above. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

These providers operate servers that may be located outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area and outside Türkiye. By using the game you understand that your data can be transferred to and processed in such countries. Where required, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer under applicable data protection law.

Retention periods

  • Cloud Save data and your anonymous player ID: until you delete your account.
  • Purchase records (store transaction ID linked to the player ID): kept after account deletion for as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention rules require. Purchase records held by Google follow Google’s own rules.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the game, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to: know whether we process your data and receive a copy; have wrong or incomplete data corrected; have your data deleted; restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable form; withdraw consent you have given; and complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights write to huseyin.cinar@cinar-works.com. We reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the owner of the player ID.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all saved game data in the game under Settings → Delete Account, or by e-mail. All routes are explained on the Data Deletion page.

Children and minimum age

The game is for players aged 13 or older. You confirm your age before you start playing. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the game, write to huseyin.cinar@cinar-works.com and we will delete the data. If you are under the age of majority in your country, make purchases only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we publish a new version number at the bottom of this page and the game asks you to accept it again.
