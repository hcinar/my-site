Deleting Your Data

Three routes to remove your account and your game data.

1. In the game (recommended)

Open the game and go to Settings → Delete Account. You need to be online. The game then deletes, in this order: your Cloud Save game data, your anonymous player (the Unity Authentication account, including the link to a Unity Player Account if you signed in), and all data stored on your device. If any online step fails, nothing on your device is removed and the game tells you, so you can try again. After deletion the game behaves like a fresh install.

2. By e-mail (without opening the game)

Write to huseyin.cinar@cinar-works.com with the subject “Delete my data” and include your player ID (see below). We may ask you to confirm that you own the ID before we delete anything, for example by sending the request from the e-mail address you use for your Google Play account or by telling us a purchase order number. Use this route if you cannot open the game, for example because you already uninstalled it.

3. Removing data from your device only

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in your phone’s settings removes only the data stored on the device. It does not delete your Cloud Save data or your player ID; use route 1 or 2 for that.

Deleting your Unity Player Account

If you signed in with Google, Apple or e-mail, that sign-in is a Unity Player Account. It belongs to Unity, not to us: deleting your game account removes its link to the game, but the Unity Player Account itself stays. To delete it as well, use Unity’s page player-account.unity.com/delete-account. For other data deletion or disclosure requests about your Unity Player Account, write to Unity at unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.

Finding your player ID

In the game open Settings. Your player ID is shown in the account section with a Copy button. Paste it into your e-mail.

Response time

We confirm and carry out deletion requests sent by e-mail within 30 days at the latest. In-app deletion takes effect immediately.

What is deleted and what is retained

  • Deleted: your Cloud Save game data (progress, settings, premium status), your anonymous player ID, the link to your sign-in identity, and the data on your device.
  • Retained: purchase records (the store transaction ID linked to the player ID) are kept for as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention rules require. Purchase records held by Google follow Google’s rules.
  • Not ours to delete: your Unity Player Account itself (the Google, Apple or e-mail sign-in) belongs to Unity; we only remove its link to the game. To delete it, use player-account.unity.com/delete-account (see above).
  • Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your data. Cancel them in your Google Play subscription settings.
